# `wiki/decision-logs/`

One page per decision that is worth being able to look up later.

- Start from `decision-log.md` in `product-building-tools/templates/`. `type: decision`.
- Name the file after the decision, not the meeting: `exports-stay-csv-only.md`, not `march-planning-sync.md`.
- The page states what was decided, what it rules out, and why. Who decided it goes in the body, not in frontmatter.
- Link the product docs the decision changes, and the meeting log it came out of, as relative wikilinks under `## Related`.
- A decision that is later reversed goes `status: deprecated` with a pointer to the one that replaced it. Do not edit the original to say something else.
