# `wiki/competitor-notes/`

What another product does, observed and dated.

- Start from `competitor-note.md` in `product-building-tools/templates/`. `type: competitor-note`.
- `competitor` is required in frontmatter: the company or product observed, for example `a named competitor`.
- Every claim about the outside world carries a source URL in the body. Label anything inferred as inference, not observation.
- `lastModified` matters more here than anywhere else: a competitor's pricing or policy goes stale quietly. Update it on every edit.
- Describe what they do. Do not write what we should do about it; that belongs in a PRD or a decision log.
