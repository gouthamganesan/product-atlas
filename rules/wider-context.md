# Research, roadmap, news and other context

Research separates evidence, sources and synthesis. Roadmap pages distinguish commitments from ideas and link to the relevant PRDs and decisions. News is dated and sourced. Miscellaneous pages are references with a clear owner or purpose; move them to a specific section when one fits.

Use existing schema types (`research` or `reference`), required frontmatter and relative links. Keep unresolved questions visible. A chat message or meeting mention is not by itself an approved product decision. Follow `rules/meeting-logs.md` for recording restrictions.
