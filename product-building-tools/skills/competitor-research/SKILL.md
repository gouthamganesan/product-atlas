# Competitor Research

Paths below are relative to the Atlas workspace root. Read `AGENTS.md` first.

Read `rules/competitor-notes.md` and the competitor-note template. Identify the requested competitors and time window. Use available browsing, APIs or supplied exports; cite the original source URL and observation date for each claim.

Compare with the last dated record. Distinguish a newly observed change from a confirmed release and label inferences. Separate observed behaviour from product recommendations. Note inaccessible or stale sources rather than filling gaps.

There is no finished competitor-update CLI in this starter. Do not claim automated monitoring exists. Store only the requested research. Scheduling, account connections or sending summaries require their own authorization.
