# Capture Meeting

Paths below are relative to the Atlas workspace root. Read `AGENTS.md` first.

Read `rules/meeting-logs.md` and the meeting-log template. Use supplied notes, or a connected meeting/chat integration within the requested scope. Teams, Cliq, Slack, Wispr Flow and equivalent sources are optional; discover available capabilities rather than assuming specific tool names.

Separate discussion, confirmed decisions, unresolved questions and actions. Preserve dates, explicit owners and known due dates. Mark inferred attribution and contradictions. Do not invent agreement from a chat thread or silently resolve open loops.

Summarize rather than copying raw transcripts; follow the rule against recorder links in shared pages. Link the relevant PRD, decision record or product document. Identify documentation affected by a discussion without changing its meaning on the basis of an unconfirmed decision. Only update additional records within the user's authorized scope. Report unavailable sources.
